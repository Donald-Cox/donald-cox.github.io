Privacy

This site has no contact forms, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cookies. Apart from the question panel described below, it does not ask for or store anything about you. The pages load their fonts and scripts from this site and make no requests to anyone else. The demos run in your browser on made-up data and send nothing anywhere.

The question panel

The home page has an "Ask about my work" panel. It stays closed unless you open it. What you type and the earlier turns of that conversation go to a small service I run on Cloudflare, which sends your questions to Anthropic so a Claude model can write the answer. Please don't type personal details. My service does not store your questions. It keeps token counts, timing and the outcome of each request, plus a scrambled form of your IP address for up to two days to limit how often the panel can be used. Your conversation stays in that browser tab and is gone when you leave the page. Anthropic handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Cloudflare may also log requests to that service, including IP addresses, to run and secure it. If I have switched the panel off on this site, nothing you type leaves your browser.

What the host may log

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits, including IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure. I don't control those logs and I don't receive them. GitHub explains this in its privacy statement.

If you email me

The email links open your own mail program. I will have what you send, and I will use it only to answer you.
